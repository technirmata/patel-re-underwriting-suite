      HOW IT WORKS
      You take title via deed. The seller's existing mortgage stays in their name. You make payments on their behalf. No qualifying required. Due-on-sale clause risk.

      SELLER FINANCE — STACKED LOANS
      Seller carries notes in one or more positions. Add up to 3 loans (e.g., 1st position bank loan + 2nd position seller carry, or all-seller-finance). Each loan has its own rate, amort period, and balloon term.

      🔑 Lease-Option / Rent-to-Own
      HOW IT WORKS
      Tenant pays option fee (non-refundable) for the right to buy at a locked strike price. Monthly rent credit accumulates toward down payment. If they walk, you keep option fee + credits.

      ⚡ Deal Inputs — Morby Method
      🏠 Property
      📋 Seller Carry (~50% of Purchase)
      Seller finances their equity as a note. Often 0% interest — they get monthly cash flow with no tax hit. Typically 50% of purchase price.
      🏦 Bank / DSCR Loan (~50-75% of ARV)
      DSCR lender bases loan on appraised value. If 75% LTV > purchase price → difference is cash back to you for soft costs or rehab.
