      HOW IT WORKS
      Short-term asset-based loan from private lenders. High rate (10–12%), low LTV (65–75%), 6–24 month term. No income docs — they underwrite the deal, not you. Used for fix-and-flip or quick close, then refinance into conventional/DSCR at exit.

      ⚠ About this strategy
      Velocity banking uses a line of credit (typically a HELOC) to make principal "chunks" against a higher-interest mortgage, then aggressively pays down the LOC using monthly cash flow. The math here is faithful to that model. Important caveats: HELOCs are usually variable-rate — if the LOC rate climbs above the mortgage rate, the strategy can lose money. Most independent analyses show real-world savings are modest vs. simply applying the same monthly surplus directly to the mortgage as extra principal. Use the Mortgage Extra Payment comparison row below before committing to a HELOC strategy.
