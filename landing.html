— 02 / Thesis

Most deals die in the gaps
your pro-forma never modeled.

Tax-record mismatches kill the appraisal. Lenders ghost on creative finance. Pro-formas lie about T-12 rent. The default tooling — spreadsheets stitched together at midnight — wasn't built for the speed deals actually move at. This is the field guide we wished existed when we were closing our first ten.

№ 02.1 — Appraisal gap

The valuation never matched the seller's deck.

In-place T-12 rent vs. lease roll vs. market rent — three different stories. Appraiser comes in $80k low. You're renegotiating 14 days from close. Patel RE flags T-12 / lease-roll / market spread before you sign the LOI.

№ 02.2 — Tax reassessment

Property taxes recalibrate at sale.

The seller's pro-forma showed taxes at $4,200. The county reassesses at sale price; your DSCR drops below 1.20 and the lender pulls. We pull the public tax record, model the post-sale reassessment, and surface the new DSCR before underwriting locks.

№ 02.3 — Capital stack drift

Three-loan stacks need real waterfall math.

1st-position bank + 2nd-position seller carry + private money on the rehab — each with its own amort, balloon, and rate. Most spreadsheets get the cumulative debt service wrong by year three. We don't.

№ 02.4 — Decision velocity

Sellers want LOIs by Friday.

By the time your sheet has the answer, the next investor is already in DD. Every metric in this suite recomputes on every keystroke. You decide in minutes, not in a Sunday model-build.

— 06 / FAQ

Questions investors actually ask.

06.1

Will my card get charged during the trial?

No. Stripe holds the card on file but doesn't charge until day eight. We email you 48 hours before. Cancel anytime in one click via the Manage Billing menu — even 30 seconds before charge. No charge, no awkward dispute.

06.2

Why require a card to start a trial?

Filters out tire-kickers and lifts trial-to-paid conversion 2–3×. The people in the app are working on real deals, which keeps the AI chat memory and feature focus relevant. It's the same playbook Linear, Mercury, and Pace use.

06.3

Who is this for? Who is it not for?

For: active operators doing 5–100 unit deals, BRRRR, creative finance, value-add. Working syndicators raising LP money. House-hackers stepping into 4-plexes. Not for: passive Roofstock browsers, REIT investors, institutional acquirers (use Argus or Yardi for 250+ unit deals).

06.4

Are my deals kept private?

Yes. Deals are encrypted at rest and gated by row-level security in Postgres — we and other users can't read them. Never shared with lenders, sellers, or any third party. We don't train any model on your data.

06.5

What's the refund policy?

30 days, no questions. Email us within 30 days of any charge and we refund in full. We'd rather refund a one-month try-out than have you stuck on a tool that doesn't fit your workflow.

06.6

Can I bring my own AI API key?

Yes — and we recommend it. Anthropic Claude (best for analysis), Perplexity (best for live web research), OpenAI, or Groq. Drop your key in Settings, pay your AI provider directly, get unlimited deal queries with no markup.

06.7

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your deals stay accessible for 30 days after cancellation in case you re-subscribe. After 30 days they're permanently deleted. Export everything as PDF or JSON anytime from the Projects menu.

06.8

Does it work on mobile?

Read-only review on mobile (deal recap, AI chat, viewing past deals). Heavy underwriting and the syndication waterfall builder need real screen real estate — desktop / iPad-class is the recommendation.
