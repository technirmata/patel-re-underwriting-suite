Questions investors actually ask.

Will my card get charged during the 7-day trial?
No. Stripe holds the card on file but doesn't charge until day 8. We email you 2 days before charge. If you cancel before day 8. even 30 seconds before. there's no charge. Click Manage billing from your account menu to cancel.
Why do you require a card for the trial?
Two reasons: (1) it filters out tire-kickers so the people in the app are actually working on deals, which means the AI deal chat memory and feature requests stay relevant; (2) you can keep working through day 8 without re-entering payment info if you want to stay. Card collection cuts trial signups but lifts trial-to-paid conversion 2-3x. fewer dropoffs at the moment of truth.
Who is this built for? Who is it NOT for?
For: active investors across every residential and commercial asset class: multifamily (from a 4-plex to a 1,000+ unit institutional syndication), SFR / 1–4 unit portfolios, short-term rentals, mobile home parks, RV parks, self-storage, mixed-use, retail strips, office, NNN commercial, and land / development sites. No deal-size cap. same metrics, same agents, same waterfall whether you're modeling a duplex BRRRR or a 800-door value-add across three properties. Strategies covered: BRRRR, subject-to, Morby, seller-finance (straight and wrap), lease-options, syndication, JV / equity partnership, velocity banking, all-cash, value-add. House-hackers stepping into 4-plexes. Working syndicators raising LP money. Not for: passive Roofstock-marketplace browsers and REIT investors. Those are vehicles, not deals to underwrite. Everything else is fair game.
Are my deals kept private?
Yes. Each user's deals are encrypted at rest and gated by row-level security in our database. we (and other users) can't read them. Your deals are never shared with lenders, sellers, or any third party. We don't train any model on your data.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your deals stay accessible for 30 days after cancellation in case you re-subscribe. After 30 days they're permanently deleted. You can export everything as PDF or JSON anytime from the Projects menu.
Can I bring my own AI API key?
Yes. and we recommend it. The AI deal chat works with Anthropic Claude (best for analysis), Perplexity (best for live web research), OpenAI, or Groq. Drop your key in Settings, pay your AI provider directly, get unlimited deal queries with no markup from us.
What's the refund policy?
30 days, no questions asked. Email us within 30 days of any charge and we'll refund it in full. We'd rather refund a one-month try-out than have you stuck on a tool that doesn't fit.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes for read-only review (deal recap, AI chat, viewing past deals) and basic intake. Heavy underwriting + the partner waterfall builder is a desktop-class experience. large tables and side-by-side comparisons need real screen real estate.
